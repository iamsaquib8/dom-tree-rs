# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, and critique code and
ideas rather than people. Harassment, insults, and personal or political
attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any other
project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer
privately at the email address listed in `Cargo.toml` (`authors`). Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and
may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for repeated or serious
violations, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
