Trailhead 28L Daypack

$129.00

4.7 out of 5 (212 reviews)

The Trailhead 28 is the pack we reach for on long day hikes, scrambles and the occasional hut-to-hut overnight. It carries a full day of water, food and layers without feeling like a backpacking pack, and it packs down small enough to stuff into a duffel.

A ventilated, suspended mesh back panel keeps air moving between you and the load, and the removable hip belt takes weight off your shoulders on longer climbs. The main compartment opens wide with a U-shaped zip, so you are not digging for the jacket at the bottom.

  • Recycled 210D ripstop body with a water-resistant coating
  • Hydration sleeve and hose ports on both shoulders
  • Two stretch side pockets that fit a one-litre bottle
  • Trekking pole loops and an ice axe loop
